# D. TORQUE: THE KING'S GAME

## Prologue

Police called it an accident.

A body was discovered in an empty field.

Detective D. Torque noticed something everyone else missed.

The backpack wasn't luggage.

It was a parachute.

The victim had fallen from the sky.

Case #1 solved.

But it was only the beginning.

---

## Chapter 1: The Six Victims

Over the next year, six mysterious deaths appeared across the city.

Different locations.

Different methods.

Different victims.

Police saw no connection.

Torque saw a pattern.

Every victim had crossed paths years earlier.

Every victim possessed part of a secret.

Someone was eliminating them one by one.

---

## Chapter 2: The Board

Torque covered an entire wall with photographs.

Red string connected names.

Dates.

Locations.

Phone numbers.

The evidence formed a single shape.

A crown.

At the center stood one name.

Victor Hale.

---

## Chapter 3: The King's Game

Victor Hale had spent years manipulating powerful people.

When someone learned too much, they disappeared.

Each victim carried one piece of evidence.

None knew the full truth.

Together, their secrets exposed Hale's empire.

Someone was trying to keep the puzzle unsolved forever.

---

## Chapter 4: Final Move

Torque followed the clues through abandoned warehouses, hidden bank accounts, and forgotten records.

The final evidence revealed everything.

The six victims had been silenced.

The deaths were connected.

Victor Hale was responsible.

The King had finally been found.

---

## Epilogue

The city celebrated the case being closed.

But Torque knew better.

On his desk sat a new file.

A new mystery.

A new game.

THE END
