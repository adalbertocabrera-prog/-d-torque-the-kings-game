D. TORQUE: THE KING’S GAME

Season 1

Episode 1: The Falling Man

A body is discovered in an open field. Police rule it an accident, but Detective D. Torque notices something unusual: the victim’s backpack is actually a parachute. The investigation reveals the victim fell from the sky and was carrying information connected to a larger conspiracy.

⸻

Episode 2: Harbor Shadows

A dock worker is found dead at the harbor. Evidence points to a simple robbery, but Torque uncovers signs that the victim was secretly meeting someone connected to a powerful organization. Another piece of the puzzle emerges.

⸻

Episode 3: Room 308

A mysterious death inside Room 308 of an abandoned hotel appears impossible. No signs of forced entry exist. As Torque reconstructs the victim’s final hours, he discovers hidden documents that link the case to previous victims.

⸻

Episode 4: The Witness

A key witness disappears before testifying. Torque races against time to locate them while mysterious forces attempt to erase all evidence. The witness reveals the name that appears in every investigation.

⸻

Episode 5: Hale Industries

The trail leads directly to Victor Hale and his corporation. Torque uncovers years of corruption, cover-ups, and secret operations. The walls begin closing in as Hale realizes someone is finally connecting the cases.

⸻

Episode 6: The King’s Game

All six cases converge. Torque assembles every clue, every witness statement, and every hidden document. The truth behind The King’s Game is exposed, leading to a final confrontation with Victor Hale.

⸻

Season Finale

Victor Hale is revealed as the mastermind behind The King’s Game. The six victims each possessed part of a secret that could destroy his empire. With the conspiracy exposed, Torque solves the greatest case of his career.

END OF SEASON 1
