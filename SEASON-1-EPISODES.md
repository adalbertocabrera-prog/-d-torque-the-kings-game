# D. Torque: The King's Game
## Season 1

Episodes

1. The Falling Man
2. Harbor Shadows
3. Room 308
4. The Witness
5. Hale Industries
6. The King's Game

---

### Episode 1: The Falling Man
A body is discovered in a field. Police believe it was an accident until Detective D. Torque notices a backpack that is actually a parachute.

### Episode 2: Harbor Shadows
A death at the docks appears unrelated. D. Torque discovers connections to a larger network.

### Episode 3: Room 308
A locked hotel room contains clues pointing toward a hidden organization.

### Episode 4: The Witness
A surviving witness reveals information that links multiple victims together.

### Episode 5: Hale Industries
The investigation leads to the powerful corporation Hale Industries and its mysterious owner.

### Episode 6: The King's Game
D. Torque uncovers the truth about Victor Hale and the conspiracy connecting all six cases.
